About this database

This database catalogs reports of successful genetic transformation in Chlorophyta (green algae) and Ochrophyta — including diatoms, eustigmatophytes, xanthophytes, and brown algae — drawn from the primary scientific literature. Entries document the species, strain, delivery method, selectable marker or reporter, and — where reported — the quantified transformation efficiency, exactly as stated by the original authors.

Efficiency metrics are heterogeneous across studies (transformants per µg DNA, transformants per 106 cells, percentage of positive cells, editing frequency, etc.) and are not directly comparable between entries; always consult the original paper for experimental context. Entries without a retrievable quantified value are landmark transformation reports retained for completeness and can be hidden with the efficiency filter. Some CRISPR-based entries report genome-editing or homologous-recombination frequencies rather than stable-transformation rates; this is noted per entry.

Scope note: organisms outside Chlorophyta and Ochrophyta (e.g., cyanobacteria, red algae) are excluded. A small number of efficiency values were sourced from citing peer-reviewed papers where the original was paywalled; these are flagged in the entry notes.
